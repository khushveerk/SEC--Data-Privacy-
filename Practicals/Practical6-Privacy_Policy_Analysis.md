# Practical 6: Privacy Policy Analysis of GeeksforGeeks

## Aim
To analyze the Privacy Policy of GeeksforGeeks and identify how it collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects users' personal data.

### Organization
GeeksforGeeks is an online technical education platform that provides programming tutorials, coding problems, courses, contests, and other educational services. Its Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used.

### Privacy Policy Analysis
| Aspect                 | Analysis                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Data Collection        | GFG may collect name, email, phone number, IP address, device information, usage data and location data.                                                       |
| Purpose                | Data is used to provide, improve, personalize and maintain its services.                                                                                       |
| Cookies                | Cookies and tracking technologies are used for preferences, security, analytics and advertising.                                                               |
| Data Sharing           | Information may be shared with service providers and other third parties in specified circumstances.                                                           |
| Data Retention         | Data is generally retained while an account is active and is stated to be deleted within 15 days after account deletion, subject to exceptions.                |
| User Rights            | Users may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, objection and data portability.                                                                    |
| Security               | GFG states that reasonable measures are used to protect personal data, while acknowledging that no online transmission or storage method is completely secure. |
| International Transfer | Personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the user's jurisdiction.                                                                |

These points are based on the information in GFG's published Privacy Policy.

### Privacy Gaps Identified
A large amount of user and usage information may be collected.
Cookies and advertising technologies can track user activity.
Third-party services may process user information.
Some user activity may be visible to other users or shared with potential recruiters.

### Recommendations
1. Collect only necessary personal information.
2. Provide simple and clear privacy settings.
3. Clearly explain third-party data sharing.
4. Allow users to easily manage cookies and tracking.
5. Regularly review data retention and deletion practices.

### Result

The Privacy Policy of GeeksforGeeks was successfully analyzed, and its data collection, usage, sharing, retention, security, and user-rights practices were identified.

### Conclusion

GeeksforGeeks provides detailed information about its privacy practices and offers several user rights and controls. However, users should remain aware of cookies, third-party services, public activity, and the information they provide while using the platform.
