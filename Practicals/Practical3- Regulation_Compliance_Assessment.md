# Practical 3: Regulation Compliance Assessment of GeeksforGeeks

## Aim-
To examine the privacy regulations applicable to GeeksforGeeks and assess how its privacy practices address important legal and regulatory requirements related to personal data protection.

### Organization

GeeksforGeeks is an online technical education platform that provides programming tutorials, courses, coding contests, and other educational services. It collects and processes user information while providing these services.

### Applicable Privacy Regulations
The major privacy regulations relevant to GeeksforGeeks include:

#### 1. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 –
applicable to processing of digital personal data in India where its requirements apply.
#### 2. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) – 
relevant to the processing of personal data of individuals covered by the GDPR.
#### 3. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) – 
relevant where its applicability requirements are met.

GeeksforGeeks' Privacy Policy contains specific sections addressing GDPR and CCPA and describes rights such as objection, portability, deletion, and access to information.

### Compliance Assessment
| Requirement            | GeeksforGeeks Practice                                                                           | Status   |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| Data Collection        | Collects personal and usage data and describes the categories collected                          | safe     |
| Purpose of Processing  | States purposes such as providing and improving services                                         | safe     |

| Data Security          | States that reasonable measures are used to protect data                                         | safe     |
