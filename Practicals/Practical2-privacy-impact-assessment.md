# Aim -To conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) of an online student attendance system and identify potential privacy risks and suitable measures to mitigate them.

## System Selected

### Online Student Attendance Management System

### Requirements
Internet connection
Web browser
Basic knowledge of data privacy

### Introduction
- A Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) is a process used to identify and evaluate privacy risks associated with a system that collects or processes personal data.

### Procedure

Identify the personal data collected by the system.
Identify the purpose of collecting the data.
Determine who can access the data.
Identify possible privacy risks.
Assess the likelihood and impact of each risk.
Suggest measures to reduce or eliminate the risks.
Data Collected

#### The system may collect:

Student name
Roll number
Attendance records
Email/phone number
Course and class information

| Data/Risk                               | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Mitigation                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | -------- | --------------------------------- |
| Unauthorized access to student records  | Medium     | High   |  High    | Access control and authentication |
| Leakage of attendance data              | Medium     | Medium |  Medium  | Encryption                        |
| Unnecessary collection of personal data | Medium     | Medium |  Medium  | Data minimization                 |
| Data kept for too long                  | Low        | Medium |  Low     | Define retention period           |
| Incorrect student information           | Medium     | Medium |  Medium  | Allow correction and verification |

### Privacy Protection Measures
1. Use strong authentication for teachers and administrators.
2. Encrypt sensitive student information.
3. Give users access only to the data they need.
4. Collect only necessary information.
5. Define a proper data retention and deletion policy.
6. Regularly review the system for privacy risks.


### Result
The Privacy Impact Assessment of the Online Student Attendance Management System was successfully performed, and potential privacy risks and their mitigation measures were identified.

### Conclusion
The PIA shows that student attendance systems can involve several privacy risks, particularly unauthorized access and data leakage. Implementing access control, encryption, data minimization, and proper retention policies can help protect students' personal information.
