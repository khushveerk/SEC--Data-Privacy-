# Practical 1: Data Privacy Audit of GeeksforGeeks

## Aim - To conduct a data privacy audit of the GeeksforGeeks website and identify how user data is collected, used, stored, and protected.

### Website Selected

#### Organization: GeeksforGeeks
#### Website: GeeksforGeeks⁠
#### Privacy Policy: GFG Privacy Policy⁠

### Requirements

Internet connection
Web browser
GeeksforGeeks account (optional)

### Procedure

• Visit the GeeksforGeeks website.

• Open its Privacy Policy.

• Identify the types of personal information collected.

• Check how the information is used.

• Examine cookies and tracking technologies.

• Check third-party services and data sharing.

• Examine available privacy and security controls.

• Identify possible privacy risks.

• Suggest measures to improve privacy.

### Observations:-
| Privacy Aspect       | Observation                                                                                         | Risk   |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Data Collection      | Name, email, phone number, IP address and usage data may be collected.                              | Medium |
| Data Usage           | Data is used to provide, improve and personalize services.                                          | Low    |
| Cookies              | Cookies and tracking technologies are used for functionality, analytics and advertising.            | Medium |
| Third-party Services | Data may be shared with service providers and other third parties.                                  | Medium |
| Profile Visibility   | User profiles and some activities may be visible to other users.                                    | Medium |
| Security             | Reasonable measures are used to protect personal data.                                              | Low    |
| Data Deletion        | Personal data is stated to be deleted within 15 days after account deletion, subject to exceptions. | Low    |



These observations are based on GFG's published Privacy Policy. 
GeeksforGeeks

### Privacy Risks Identified

• Personal information may be exposed through public profiles or activities.
• Cookies and advertising technologies can track user activity.
• Third-party services may process some user information.
• GFG states that some coding activity, course progress and achievements may be shared with potential recruiters. 
GeeksforGeeks


### Recommendations

• Users should avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.
• Review privacy settings and profile visibility.
• Review permissions given to third-party services.
• Avoid putting sensitive information in public posts or profiles.
• Read the Privacy Policy before using additional services.

### Result
The Data Privacy Audit of GeeksforGeeks was successfully performed. The audit identified its data collection, usage, cookies, third-party sharing, security measures and potential privacy risks.

### Conclusion
GeeksforGeeks provides a Privacy Policy and several measures for protecting user data. However, users should remain careful about public information, cookies, third-party services and the information they provide on the platform.
